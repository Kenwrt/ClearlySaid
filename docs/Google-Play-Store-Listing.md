## Full description

ClearlySaid helps you say what you mean with less effort.

Dictate naturally or type a rough message, then let ClearlySaid return a clearer, more concise version that is ready to copy and send. It is useful for emails, follow-ups, requests, difficult conversations, and everyday messages.

Free features:

* Dictate or type a message
* Improve clarity, grammar, spelling, and flow
* Copy the refined message with one tap
* Start over quickly with the Clear button
* Use up to 20 message refinements each month

Paid plans also let you shape the result by choosing its purpose, tone, and directness. Create combinations such as Professional + Balanced, Warm and friendly + Gentle, or Firm + Direct.

ClearlySaid processes message text only to produce the requested rewrite. Message contents are not saved in the account database. Microphone audio is handled by speech-recognition services available on your device, and ClearlySaid receives the resulting text.

Say it naturally. Send it clearly.
